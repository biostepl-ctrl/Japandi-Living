Privacy Policy

 

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

At Japandi Living, accessible through our website, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you may share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Japandi Living may collect certain information when you browse our website, including:

  • Personal Information: Information you voluntarily provide, such as your name or email address when contacting us or subscribing to updates.

  • Non-Personal Information: General information about your browser, device, operating system, pages visited, and browsing activity.

  • Cookies and Usage Data: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies to improve website performance and understand visitor preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Improve our website content, design, and user experience.

  • Respond to questions, comments, and inquiries.

  • Send newsletters or updates when you have chosen to receive them.

  • Analyze website traffic and understand which interior design topics interest our readers.

  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Japandi Living may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, analyze website traffic, and improve functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

If we use third-party analytics or advertising services, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, products, services, or resources related to interior design, home decor, and furniture.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information or making purchases.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website, analyze performance, or maintain security.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect our rights, website, or visitors.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that information is handled appropriately by service providers.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to help protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.

  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.

  • Request deletion of your personal information.

  • Withdraw consent to certain data processing activities.

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing where applicable.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.

8. Children's Privacy

Japandi Living is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
